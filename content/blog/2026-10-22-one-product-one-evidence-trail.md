The existing BiasLens repository is the integration target for the broader truth-seeking product. TruthSeeker's first article-analysis slice has already been brought across. Its donor history remains available for comparison and rollback. A later standalone interface experiment is superseded rather than becoming another product to maintain.

**BiasLens is the product name.** Development stays in its existing repository,
with the same hosting and project identifiers. The broader scope does not
require a second app or a rebrand.

## Explore more than an article

The [question workspace](/explore) lets you keep a question, its scope, source locations, annotations and challenges together. It is browser-local. Its export is a research record, not a certified answer; URLs and excerpts are supplied by the user and remain unverified.

The [article workspace](/analyze) examines the structure of a supplied text using the configured model provider. Independent multi-source verification is a different capability and is not claimed. The Debate Arena measures timed speaker turns supplied by a user; it does not authenticate a recording or determine whether an overlap was an interruption.

## Put the specification where people can inspect it

The [technical white paper](/whitepaper) explains the model, source independence, causal reasoning, framing dimensions, evaluation requirements and failure cases. It is a new working document, not a silently reconstructed copy of an unavailable historical blueprint.

The [development index](/development) points to the canonical project profile, reviewed changes, journal and challenge channel. Roadmap and changelog are not hand-copied into another page. The existing bip-kit publishing package reads the papers and articles; no parallel content engine is introduced.

## Accountability starts with our own status labels

Working source code, passing tests, a successful deployment and a valid research method are four different things. We should show the evidence for each. The product cannot demand traceable claims from others while treating its own intentions as delivered capabilities.
