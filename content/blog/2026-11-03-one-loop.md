BiasLens had too many doors that sounded the same: examine, investigate, rewrite, notebook. We're folding them into one loop.

**Your feed.** Follow the outlets you read, CNN, Fox News, Weltwoche, Republik or anyone else. When an article is behind a paywall we show the headline, and we can write our own labelled article from it and from the evidence.

**One page per article, four modes.**

- **Read** the original.
- **Talk** to it: explain it, dig deeper, what's missing, who disagrees.
- **Check** its claims and gaps. If a story says accidents in Zurich rose by X% and stops there, we ask what it skipped: more traffic, more cyclists, a change in how accidents get counted. Then we look for evidence on each. During COVID, outlets called reliable repeated claims about masks and lockdowns that lacked high-quality trials. That is the kind of gap we go looking for.
- **Rewrite** it in the style or perspective you choose. Every rewrite is labelled.

**Pick your bias, and know it.** If you want news through a particular lens, you can have it. You'll always see which lens you're using and can flip to neutral or the opposite view in one tap. That beats trusting that an outlet has no bias when it does.

**Outlets earn their record.** We don't put a bias score on each article. Over time, outlets get a track record from how their articles hold up under our published method. The results may not match the usual lists of which sources are reliable.

The full plan is in our roadmap. Building starts with the single article page.
